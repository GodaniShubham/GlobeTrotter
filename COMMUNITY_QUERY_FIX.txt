Fixed:
  TypeError: Cannot reorder a query once a slice has been taken.

Cause:
  popular_posts queryset was sliced with [:50] before _annotated_posts()
  and then reordered with order_by(...).

Correct pattern:
  1. Build the unsliced queryset.
  2. Annotate it.
  3. Apply the final order_by().
  4. Slice the final result.

Applied to both community feed and post detail popular-conversation queries.
