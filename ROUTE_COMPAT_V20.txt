Route compatibility V20

Fixes:
  - /trip/builder/?trip=<id> manual stop add route now resolves.
  - /trip/budget/?trip=<id> now works with budget(request, trip_id) signatures.
  - /trip/calendar/?trip=<id> supports both calendar(request) and calendar(request, trip_id).
  - /trip/itinerary/?trip=<id> supports both itinerary signatures.
  - community notifications/share/reply/like/save routes are wired to community.views.
  - current trip-specific URLs are retained.
  - no migrations required.

Replace config/urls.py, then run:
  python manage.py check
  python manage.py runserver
